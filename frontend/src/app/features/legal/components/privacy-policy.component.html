This policy explains what information Resonate collects, why, and how you can ask about or remove it. It covers what the site actually does today — it'll be updated if that changes (for example, if analytics or advertising are added).

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, first and last name, username, a hashed password (we never store your password in plain text), and an optional bio and avatar image.
  • Content you create: posts, comments, likes, and follows.
  • Sign-in state: your access and refresh tokens are stored in your browser's local storage (not cookies) so you stay signed in between visits.

We don't currently use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking scripts.

How we use it

  • To operate your account and show your posts, comments, and profile to other readers.
  • To send email notifications when someone comments on your post or follows you — only if you've left those notification preferences turned on in your profile settings.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the site, and your data passes through them as part of that:

  • Supabase — hosts our database.
  • Render — hosts our backend API.
  • Firebase Hosting (Google) — hosts the website you're reading now.
  • Resend — delivers the email notifications described above.

We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile information, avatar, and notification preferences any time from your account settings.
  • Delete individual posts and comments yourself, any time.
  • Contact us (below) to request a copy of your data, a correction, or full account deletion.

Children's privacy

Resonate isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: privacy@resonate.blog
